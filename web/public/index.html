🏛️ Assam History

The Ahom Dynasty: An Overview

The Ahom dynasty ruled Assam for nearly 600 years (1228 – 1826) and played a key role in shaping the region's political, cultural and social identity. They successfully resisted several Mughal attempts to capture Assam and established a unique administrative system.
